01 / THE GARAGE IN MY HEADSome dreams come with four wheels.
+

The BMW M4 F82 made me a car person. The Cruze made the obsession personal. Some cars stay in your head long before they’re in your garage.

I’ll reach home and spend forty more minutes in the car because the playlist is still doing its thing. You get it.

Jibhi. Nainital. A quieter version of my head. The problems wait; I usually return a little lighter.

Cricket was part of my life before it became part of my work. The opinions, unfortunately, have always been free.

Being loved. Being understood. Having people who knew you before any of this. Still the biggest flex.

The place where failing a rep can mean you’re getting stronger. I’m trying to remember that outside the gym, too.
