01 THE STORY SO FAR

There isn’t a tidy
three-word version of me.

↗

For the person who wants the short version: curious, restless, and trying to pay attention.

I can spend a day questioning a metric, then leave work with a song stuck in my head, a line I want to post, an opinion about a car, and a gym session still to finish.

They all come from the same place. I like getting closer to what makes something work—a product, a sentence, a person, a life.

Some days that looks like sharp thinking. Some days it looks like thinking too much. You get both here.

01 / THE QUESTIONS

Work taught me
to look closer.

I work in product analysis. What keeps me interested is the question behind the number: what people actually did, why it happened, and what we should do next. GoMechanic, Curious Jr, Dream11, and MyNaksh have all been parts of that learning. I’m still trying to get better at the craft.

02 / THE CITY

Space changes
what home means.

Living in Bengaluru gave me room to become more myself. It also made me miss the ordinary things I used to take for granted. Turns out you can want independence and still want to go home and annoy your mother.

Forever mumma’s boy. Some things don’t change.
03 / THE PROMISE

Show up.
Do the reps.

The gym is a place where progress can be counted. Some days I feel unstoppable; some days I still have to drag myself there. I keep going. That matters more to me than a transformation montage.

No miracle routine. Just the next session.
04 / THE UNFINISHED BIT

Still becoming.
Always.

I want to do work that matters, write words that sound like me, stay close to the people I love, and keep enough curiosity for everything I haven’t tried yet. I don’t have a final version of myself to announce.

Good. It would be boring if I did.
